Python has a fun culture behind its serious power. Here are some light, cool facts you can drop in conversations.

Name and origin
Python is not named after the snake but after the British comedy group “Monty Python’s Flying Circus,” because its creator Guido van Rossum was a big fan.​

Guido started Python around Christmas 1989 as a hobby project to keep himself busy, long before it became one of the world’s most popular languages.​
